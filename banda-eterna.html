Somosbubble
Branding System
Brand manual

Somos Bubble, a space where we bet on dynamic entertainment with a fresh, youthful tone. Bubble is a vibrant bubble where anything can happen and everyone wants in.

Typography

We chose Poppins for its round, geometric shapes, which echo the curves of the logo and the idea of a bubble. It stays clear and legible on small screens, works for both big titles and short copy, and brings a friendly, modern tone without losing personality.

Color palette

The palette comes from the logo's gradient: magenta, orange and yellow carry Bubble's energy, humor and youthful tone, while white adds air and contrast so the content can breathe. Vibrant colors that stand out instantly in a busy feed.
